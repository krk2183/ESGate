# 🌟 ESGATE V2 – AI-Powered ESG Intelligence for SMEs

**A next-generation ESG improvement insights platform built for small and medium enterprises. Providing clarity, transparency, and actionable sustainability intelligence.**

---

## ✨ Highlights

* **AI-Driven Analysis:** AI-generated ESG analysis and tailored recommendations.
* **Automated Scoring:** Scoring engine based on real-world ESG frameworks.
* **Clean Visualizations:** Dashboard analytics featuring clean, intuitive data visualizations.
* **Investor-Ready:** Shareable, public-facing company profile cards for investors.
* **Curated News:** AI-curated ESG news feed with intelligent topic clustering.

---

## 🌍 Overview



ESGate v2 is the improved and finalized version of our prototype. ESGate enables companies to understand and significantly improve their **Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG)** standing with minimal friction.

We translate complex ESG principles into:

* **Appliable Feedback**
* **Clear Insights**
* **Practical Recommendations**
* **Visually Appealing Dashboards**

---

## 🧠 Core Features

### 1. ESG Scoring Engine 📊
* Multidimensional scoring across **E, S, and G pillars**.
* Custom weighted scoring formula for comprehensive assessment.
* **Benchmarking** functionality against sector averages.

### 2. AI-Generated Insights 💡
* Automatic identification of **Strengths, Weaknesses, and Risk Flags**.
* Contextual improvement recommendations.
* **Natural Language** ESG summaries.

### 3. Company Profile Cards 💳
* Public-facing, **investor-oriented preview**.
* Cleanly displayed score badges and highlight metrics.

### 4. ESG News & Trends 📰
* AI-curated news pipeline pulling from relevant sources.
* Intelligent **Topic Clustering** for quick understanding.
* **Sentiment Tagging** to gauge public perception.
