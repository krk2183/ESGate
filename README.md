# 🏆 ESGate: AI-Powered ESG Intelligence for SMEs and Youth

by **Kenan Kerimzade (krk2183)**

## 🌟 2nd Place Winner, Republic-wide Hackathon 2025 (Azerbaijan) 🌟

> "ESGate is a smart platform that blends **AI, Sustainability (ESG), and Financial Literacy**—built for small and medium businesses and young learners."

ESGate is an award-winning platform that stood out among more than 160 projects at the Republic-wide Hackathon. The judges loved its **dual-audience approach** and its innovative **AI-first take on ESG compliance**.

### 🚀 Core Mission

ESGate provides organizations with the necessary tools to achieve **European sustainability standards** while simultaneously equipping young people with **real-world business and financial skills** through hands-on, interactive learning.

### 👥 Key User Roles & Features

| User Role | Core Functionality | Highlight Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SME Accounts** | AI-driven ESG scoring, ROI optimization, compliance, and personalized improvement plans. | **AI ESG Compliance Engine** for gap analysis and actionable steps. |
| **Youth Accounts** | Hands-on, immersive business education and financial skills development. | **Virtual Business Simulator** where users manage a company and adapt to ESG trends. |
| **Investor/KOBIA** | Oversight on SME progress, tracking youth performance, and setting regional challenges. | Centralized monitoring of ESG progress and performance across the ecosystem. |

---

### 🧠 Core Intelligence & Learning

1.  **AI ESG Compliance Engine:** Automates ESG scoring and analysis, identifying gaps in EU rules and providing **clear, actionable steps**. Runs on a scalable, token-based model.
2.  **ROI Optimizer:** A **predictive tool** that uses past company data, industry context, and local regulations to recommend the smartest next ESG move for maximum return.
3.  **Personalized ESG Roadmaps:** Builds custom plans with timelines and priorities for both SMEs and investors.
4.  **Integrated Learning Environment:** Features focused **microlearning modules** (based on KOBIA materials) covering ESG basics, financial literacy, and entrepreneurship, with adaptive, real-time feedback.

---

### 🇦🇿 Alignment with KOBIA (SME Development Agency of Azerbaijan)

KOBIA, the **Small and Medium Business Development Agency of the Republic of Azerbaijan**, is a key public entity mandated to promote the growth and competitiveness of SMEs, offering training, advice, and access to finance.

* **ESGate's Synergy:** ESGate directly supports KOBIA's mission by integrating its **microlearning materials** and providing **AI-powered compliance and development tools** that are essential for increasing the competitiveness and global connection of Azerbaijani SMEs.
* **Investor & KOBIA Accounts:** These accounts are designed to facilitate KOBIA's oversight and support mechanisms, allowing them to track ESG progress and set development challenges.
