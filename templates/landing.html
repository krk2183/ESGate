The Challenge for Azerbaijani SMEs

Accessing lucrative EU capital markets requires robust Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) documentation – a significant hurdle for many growing businesses in Azerbaijan.

  • Lack of Standardization: Difficulty meeting diverse EU investor requirements.
  • Limited Resources: SMEs often lack dedicated teams for complex ESG reporting.
  • Missed Opportunities: Inability to attract sustainability-focused EU investment.

ESGate provides the tools and visibility to overcome these challenges.
